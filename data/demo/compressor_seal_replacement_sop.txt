==============================================================
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE — SOP/MEC/042
Compressor Mechanical Seal Replacement
==============================================================
Document No.: SOP/MEC/042
Revision: 03
Date: 01-Jan-2026
Plant: ONGC Mehsana Asset — Gas Processing Unit
Equipment: Centrifugal Compressor C-201 / C-202
==============================================================

1. PURPOSE
   This SOP describes the step-by-step procedure for replacing
   a faulty mechanical seal on centrifugal compressors C-201
   and C-202 in the gas processing unit.

2. SAFETY PRECAUTIONS
   2.1 Obtain a valid Permit-to-Work (PTW) before starting.
   2.2 Confirm gas-free certificate from safety department.
   2.3 Ensure Lock-Out/Tag-Out (LOTO) on all energy sources:
       - Electrical isolate at MCC panel
       - Close block valves V-201A/B (suction and discharge)
       - Vent trapped gas via vent valve V-201V
   2.4 Wear PPE: flame-retardant coverall, safety goggles,
       chemical-resistant gloves, safety shoes.
   2.5 Verify zero pressure on suction/discharge gauges.
   2.6 Stand upwind during any residual gas release.

3. TOOLS AND MATERIALS
   - Mechanical seal assembly (P/N: SEAL-C201-MK3)
   - Seal installation sleeve
   - Torque wrench (range: 20-150 Nm)
   - Dial indicator with magnetic base
   - Alignment laser tool
   - O-rings (Viton, per seal drawing)
   - Loctite 242 (medium threadlocker)
   - Clean ISO 32 lubricating oil

4. PROCEDURE

   Step 1: ISOLATION AND PREPARATION
   - Shut down compressor per shutdown procedure SOP/MEC/001.
   - Execute LOTO per SOP/SAF/005.
   - Drain process fluid from seal cavity.
   - Allow housing to cool below 40°C.

   Step 2: DISASSEMBLY
   - Remove coupling guard and coupling spacer.
   - Unbolt seal gland (8 × M16 bolts, torque: 85 Nm).
   - Carefully slide out old seal assembly.
   - Inspect shaft sleeve for scoring or corrosion.
     → If sleeve damage exceeds 0.1mm depth, replace sleeve.

   Step 3: SHAFT AND HOUSING INSPECTION
   - Measure shaft runout with dial indicator (max: 0.05mm TIR).
   - Inspect seal chamber bore for pitting or wear.
   - Clean all mating surfaces with lint-free cloth + solvent.

   Step 4: NEW SEAL INSTALLATION
   - Lubricate O-rings with clean ISO 32 oil.
   - Slide seal assembly onto shaft using installation sleeve.
   - Do NOT push seal faces together dry.
   - Torque gland bolts in star pattern: 85 Nm ± 5%.
   - Set seal compression per manufacturer spec (typically 3-5mm).

   Step 5: ALIGNMENT CHECK
   - Reinstall coupling spacer.
   - Perform laser alignment (angular: <0.05mm/100mm,
     offset: <0.05mm).
   - Record alignment readings on Form MEC/042-A.

   Step 6: LEAK TEST
   - Close vent valve V-201V.
   - Slowly open suction block valve V-201A.
   - Pressurise seal cavity to 5 bar (nitrogen).
   - Hold for 15 minutes — acceptable leak: <1 bubble/min
     at atmospheric vent.
   - If leak detected: retorque gland bolts or reseat seal.

   Step 7: STARTUP
   - Remove LOTO per SOP/SAF/005.
   - Start compressor per startup procedure SOP/MEC/002.
   - Monitor seal temperature and vibration for first 2 hours.
   - Acceptable: seal face temp <65°C, vibration <3.5mm/s RMS.

5. DOCUMENTATION
   - Complete Work Order in CMMS.
   - Record seal part numbers, shaft measurements, and
     alignment readings on Form MEC/042-A.
   - Update equipment history in SAP PM module.
   - File PTW closure with safety department.

6. REFERENCES
   - Manufacturer manual: FlowServe ISC2 Mechanical Seal
   - API 682 — Pumps — Shaft Sealing Systems
   - SOP/SAF/005 — Lock-Out / Tag-Out Procedure
   - SOP/MEC/001 — Compressor Shutdown Procedure
   - SOP/MEC/002 — Compressor Startup Procedure
