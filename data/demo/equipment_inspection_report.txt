2. INSPECTION SCOPE
   - External visual inspection
   - Ultrasonic thickness (UT) measurement — 24 grid points
   - Tube bundle eddy current testing (ECT) — 100% tubes
   - Flange face inspection
   - Nozzle weld inspection

3. FINDINGS

   3.1 EXTERNAL VISUAL
   - No visible corrosion, bulging, or distortion.
   - Insulation intact. No hot spots detected by thermal camera.
   - Foundation bolts tight. No settlement observed.

   3.2 ULTRASONIC THICKNESS (UT)
   - Shell minimum thickness: 18.2mm (nominal: 20mm, retirement: 16mm)
   - Corrosion rate: 0.15mm/year (consistent with previous readings)
   - Remaining life: ~14.7 years at current rate
   - See attached UT grid map for full readings.

   3.3 EDDY CURRENT TESTING (ECT)
   - Total tubes inspected: 324
   - Tubes with >40% wall loss: 3 (Tubes #47, #122, #289)
   - Tubes with >60% wall loss: 1 (Tube #122 — recommend plugging)
   - No through-wall defects detected.

   3.4 FLANGE FACES
   - Shell-side inlet nozzle N1: minor pitting on gasket face
     → Acceptable — re-gasket at next opportunity
   - All other flanges: satisfactory condition

   3.5 NOZZLE WELDS
   - MPI performed on N1, N2, N3, N4 nozzle welds
   - No cracks or indications found
   - All welds meet ASME Section VIII acceptance criteria

4. RECOMMENDATIONS
   [PRIORITY 1] Plug Tube #122 (>60% wall loss) before restart.
   [PRIORITY 2] Monitor Tubes #47 and #289 — re-inspect at next
                 turnaround (scheduled Mar-2027).
   [PRIORITY 3] Replace gasket on N1 nozzle flange at next
                 available maintenance window.
   [INFO]       Overall exchanger condition is SATISFACTORY.
                Next full inspection due: Sep-2028 (2-year cycle).
